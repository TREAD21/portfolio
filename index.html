About me

Meet Michael — finance, data and design in one place

I’m interested in how organisations record, report and communicate their financial and operational information — and how good design makes that information easier to act on. My work spans finance and accounting, financial reporting and dashboard development, graphic design, and building websites and apps such as Sika, BIZWISE and SchoolHub. I’m currently the Accountant / Administrator at Deseret School of Design.

I enjoy taking messy spreadsheets or loosely defined ideas and turning them into something structured, accurate and visually clear. I’m continuing to build experience across these areas and I’m open to opportunities and collaborations.

How I work,
step by step

See the work

  1. I start by learning what the numbers or content represent, who will use the result, and what decision it needs to support.

  2. Cleaning, structuring and reconciling the information so everything built on top of it is accurate.

  3. Creating the report, dashboard, graphic or web page with a clear layout and consistent visual language.

  4. Testing the result, improving it from feedback and documenting it so others can follow and maintain it.

Business finance · Web app Featured

BIZWISE — Business Finance App

An all-in-one app for small businesses: record sales with barcodes and mobile money, send invoices, track stock, customers and suppliers, and see profit and loss, a statement of financial position and profit by product. Every amount is kept in whole pesewas so totals are exact, and each team member sees only what their role allows.

Sika — Fintech App

A fintech app for Ghana: wallets, person-to-person transfers, mobile money (MTN, Telecel, AT) and bill payments. Built on a double-entry ledger that keeps every amount in exact pesewas, so balances can always be checked against their postings.
