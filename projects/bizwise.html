BIZWISE — the books, kept while you sell

A web app for small shops and businesses in Ghana. Staff record sales at the counter, stock goes down by itself, and the owner sees profit, money on hand and who owes what, without keeping a separate set of books.

Role
Product design & development
Status
Live
Platform
Web app, works on phones and computers
Stack
JavaScript · Node.js · Express · SQLite
Open BIZWISE

01The problem

Many small shops keep their records in a notebook, if at all. Money comes in as cash, Mobile Money and bank transfers, some customers buy on credit, and stock is counted by eye. At the end of the month the owner often can’t say what they actually made, or where the money went.

Accounting software exists, but it is built for accountants. I wanted something a cashier could use at the counter, that turns everyday sales into proper accounts without anyone having to post entries.

02What I built

Sales at the counter

Scan or type a barcode, take cash, Mobile Money, bank or credit, apply a promo code and print a receipt. Stock is deducted automatically.

Stock & suppliers

Products with cost and selling prices, supplier deliveries, stock adjustments and low-stock alerts.

Customers & invoices

Who owes what, with WhatsApp reminders; invoices with VAT, NHIL and GETFund; orders assigned to delivery riders.

Reports

Profit and loss, a statement of financial position, profit by product, best sellers and how money was received.

03How it works

BIZWISE is a Node.js and Express server with a SQLite database and a plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript front end, with no build step. It runs on Render, with Paystack for payments and Resend for email.

  • Exact money. Every amount is stored in whole pesewas, so totals, tax and change never pick up rounding errors.
  • One database per business. Each shop’s records live in their own database, so one business can never see another’s data.
  • Roles that match a shop. The owner sees the money; cashiers sell, inventory managers handle stock, and riders see only the orders assigned to them.
  • Ghana’s taxes built in. VAT, NHIL and GETFund are worked out per line, tax-inclusive or exclusive, as the business chooses.
  • Backups on a free plan. The server keeps no disk, so changed databases are saved to a private GitHub repository every 30 seconds and restored when it wakes.
  • Run as a service. A platform screen adds businesses, resets logins, takes backups and handles subscriptions paid by Mobile Money or card.

05What I took from it

BIZWISE is where my accounting and my code meet most directly. A sale is not just a row in a table: it moves stock, cash or a customer’s balance, and it has to land correctly in the profit and loss and the statement of financial position. Building it made me think about every transaction the way I would at month end.

Next: getting it into more shops and learning from how they use it.
